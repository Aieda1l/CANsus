## Current KiCad source snapshot

The supplied source archive is named **`CANSus Board v15.zip`**, while the PCB title block inside `CAN_board.kicad_pcb` says **`CANSus Board v14`** and dates the title block `2024-04-29`. The project files themselves were generated by **KiCad 8.0**.

Because those labels conflict with the requested v1-v6 history, the repository keeps the supplied source unchanged and does not claim that internal v14/v15 is equivalent to public revision v6.

## How to update this log

For each missing revision, add: date or season, schematic/PCB source tag, what changed electrically, what changed mechanically, why the change was made, fabrication/assembly notes, and at least one photo or render when available.
